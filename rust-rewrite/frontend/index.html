Your screenshots, finally in one place.

        
VRChat Organizer finds your VRChat captures, groups them by world, and gives you a visual library you can browse instead of a folder full of files.

        

          
          
          
        

      

      

        
How it works

        
From capture to keepsake.

        
You stay in VR. Organizer handles the tidy-up in the background, and your original screenshots remain on your computer.

        
!
Before you start: enable VRCX metadataVRCX must be installed and saving screenshot metadata must be turned on. Organizer uses that metadata to identify which VRChat world each screenshot came from.
